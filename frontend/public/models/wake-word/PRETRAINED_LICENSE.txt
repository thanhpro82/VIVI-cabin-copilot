Pretrained openWakeWord Feature Models

melspectrogram.onnx and embedding_model.onnx are vendored, unmodified
pretrained weights from the openWakeWord project (frozen; never
retrained by this repository). See the openWakeWord project for the
upstream license terms. Only vivi-wake-classifier.onnx, produced by
scripts/wake_word/train.py, is a VIVI-specific trained artifact.
